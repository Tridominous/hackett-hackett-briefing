Presenter notes

For James only · not linked from the briefing

The brief's real ask is to bring initiatives and present them credibly. These notes are how to land them. Keep this page open on a second screen or phone; present from the briefing itself.

Suggested run order (about 10 minutes)

  1. Open on the briefing hero. One line on who you are, then straight into what the company sells. 1 min
  2. Business & diagnosis. Land "trust is the product," then the four findings, Gmail first. 2 min
  3. SWOT & PESTLE. Don't read the grids. Pick two or three items each and connect them to the plan. 2 min
  4. Initiatives & the email fix. Quick wins first, then the no-lock-in principle. This is your credibility peak. 2 min
  5. Hit the button. Open the live homepage concept, toggle the theme, run the instant-quote card. 2 min
  6. Roadmap & close. Phase 0 ships in a fortnight, and you're offering to own it. 1 min

Talking points

Lead with the business, not the tech

Open by showing you understand what they sell: trust and discretion. Then frame every recommendation as protecting or growing that. Tech is the means, not the message.

Frame everything as ROI, risk or credibility

Not "let's use Astro" but "a faster site converts more high-value enquiries and costs less to run." Speak the founder's language.

Start with the cheap quick wins

The email fix and the measurement baseline show pragmatism and respect for budget. You're not a junior asking for a big rebuild on day one.

Use the concept as your moment

"I didn't just analyse it, I built you a concept." Demo it live. It's your differentiator against other candidates.

Show you understand their constraints

Name ULEZ, GDPR around VIP data, driver-status law and EV costs. It proves you researched the industry, not just the website.

Close on ownership

Offer to own the web workstream as the intern. Phase 0 could ship in your first fortnight. End with initiative, not a wish list.

The closing line

"Hackett & Hackett sells trust and discretion to an elite clientele. My job as your web developer intern is to make sure every digital touchpoint, from the homepage to the address staff email from, earns that trust before a word is exchanged. Here's how I'd do it, starting in week one."
